  2. ONE PARAGRAPH  (short description)

Spray paint on any surface in Los Santos. Press F3, pick from thirteen colours
-- including a chrome and a gold that really do read as metal -- and take a
spray can or a paint-filled fire extinguisher. Aim at anything solid
and hold the trigger -- walls, shutters, kerbs, roads, ceilings. Splatters grow
with distance and land at their own angle, so sweeping the reticle draws a real
line rather than a row of dots. Nothing runs out, your work is remembered
between sessions, and one button wipes it all. Drop-in install, no OpenIV, and
your save is never touched.

  3. CHANGELOG  (for the mod page's changelog field)

0.2.0

  THE SPRAY IS MEASURED FROM HIS HAND, NOT HIS FEET. A ped's position sits on the
  floor between its feet, so the size curve was measured from his ankles -- the
  nearest part of any wall was its skirting board, and a piece came out finest at
  the bottom and fattest at the top on a wall you were standing square to. From
  the hand it is finest across the middle where you are working and opens up
  above your reach and below it, which is what a can does. This was wrong for the
  whole of 0.1.0.

  THIRTEEN COLOURS. Chrome and gold, and they are not flat tints. Nothing in this
  game can make a mark reflective, so every mark lands in its own shade from a
  range and the scatter is what reads as metal. A grey tint is primer and a
  yellow-brown one is mustard -- that is what a "metallic" that is really a tint
  looks like.

  THREE CAPS: thin, stock, fat. A cap sets the NARROWEST line the can can draw,
  because that is what a cap is -- a fat one cannot do detail however close you
  hold it. The far end is still governed by how far off the wall you stand, so
  the three converge as you step back, exactly like real ones do.

  DENSER, AND THE RATE DIAL ACTUALLY WORKS. The can lays half again as much as it
  did. The rate had also been capped without saying so: it placed one mark per
  frame, so sixty frames a second was a hard ceiling however high the setting was
  turned.

  A NEW WORDMARK, arched and animated. It sprays itself on when the panel opens
  and glows in whatever colour is loaded.

  THE PANEL: a cap row with icons, a smaller masthead, and the on/off switch says
  MOD. F3 still opens it when the mod is switched off, so it can never lock you
  out of itself.

  IF 0.1.0 WOULD NOT LOAD FOR YOU, READ STEP 1 of the troubleshooting in the
  readme. Windows blocks DLLs that came out of a downloaded zip, and it looks
  exactly like a broken mod. Right-click the ZIP, Properties, tick Unblock, THEN
  extract.

  Smaller things: the log now says what your decal cap is and what raises it,
  paint on vehicles is attempted with a decal type that can stick to one, and the
  can reaches 2 metres measured from the can rather than 4 measured from his feet.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  4. FULL DESCRIPTION  (the mod page body)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

OVERSPRAY

Franklin gets a spray can and a fire extinguisher full of paint, and Los Santos
gets whatever you feel like putting on it.

Aim at anything solid and hold the trigger. Walls, shutters, kerbs, the road,
the side of a skip, the ceiling of a car park -- if the game will let a ray land
on it, it takes paint. Nothing is pre-placed and nothing is scripted: where you
point is where it goes.


-- IT ACTUALLY LOOKS LIKE SPRAY --

The details are the point of this one.

  * Splatters grow with distance. Up close it is a tight band you can write
    with; step back and it opens out into something you cover a garage door
    with. The spray can and the extinguisher have completely separate curves,
    because one is a can you work a foot from the wall and the other is a
    pressure vessel you stand back from.

  * Every mark lands at its own random angle. A wall covered in one blob
    repeated fifty times reads as a tiling texture, not as paint.

  * CHROME AND GOLD ARE REAL. Nothing in this game can make a mark reflective,
    so they are not done as a grey tint and a yellow tint -- which is how you
    get primer and mustard. Every mark lands in its own shade, some near white
    and some gunmetal, and the scatter reads as a reflection because that is
    the only thing that ever causes it. Costs nothing: the marks were going on
    the wall anyway.

  * Sweeping the reticle draws a CONTINUOUS LINE. The gap between one splatter
    and the next is filled in as you move, so a fast sweep is a stroke rather
    than a row of dots -- the spacing follows your hand, not a clock.

  * Paint goes on any surface at any angle. Ceilings, kerbs, the underside of a
    bridge, the bonnet of a car. It reads the surface it hit and lies flat on it.


-- TWO TOOLS --

Press F3 for the picker. Thirteen colours, and two things to spray them with:

  SPRAY CAN
      A narrow band that tightens right up close, for detail work and lettering.
      Franklin holds it properly, turns to face where he is aiming, and tags
      with the game's own graffiti animation -- and shakes the can every few
      seconds while he is standing about with it.

      THREE CAPS: thin, stock and fat. A cap sets the narrowest line the can can
      draw, which is what a cap really is -- a fat one cannot do fine work
      however close you hold it. The far end is still governed by how far off
      the wall you stand, so the three converge as you step back, exactly like
      real ones do.

  FIRE EXTINGUISHER
      Ten metres, up to two metres across, with a tinted cloud around the jet.
      For covering things quickly and making a mess.

Neither one ever runs out. An aim dot shows exactly where the paint will land,
which matters more than it sounds -- with a can in his hand the game has no
reason to draw you a reticle.


-- IT REMEMBERS --

Your work stays where you put it. Walk to the other side of the city and back
and it is still on the wall. It survives a reload. Up to fifty thousand marks
are remembered, and the ones nearest you are always the ones on screen.

CLEAR EVERY WALL wipes all of it when you want a blank canvas. It asks twice,
because there is no undo.


-- AND THE STREET NOTICES --

Passers-by do not sprint away from you like you are holding a rifle. They turn
round and tell you exactly what they think of your artwork, in their own voices.


-- SAFE TO INSTALL, EASY TO REMOVE --

  * Replaces no game files. Overwrites nothing. Never touches your save.
  * Needs no OpenIV, no mods folder, no gameconfig edit, no DLC pack.
  * The fire extinguisher is completely unmodified and still puts fires out
    exactly as it always did -- the mod only watches it and adds paint on top.
  * Uninstall by deleting four files.
  * Works on GTA V Legacy AND GTA V Enhanced.


-- IF IT DOES NOT LOAD, READ THIS FIRST --

  WINDOWS BLOCKS DLLs THAT CAME OUT OF A DOWNLOADED ZIP. This is the single most
  common reason a script mod does nothing, and it looks exactly like a broken
  mod: ScriptHookVDotNet reports an error and nothing appears in game.

  It is not the mod and it is not your install. Windows marks anything that came
  from the internet, .NET refuses to load a marked assembly, and the extracted
  files inherit the mark from the zip.

  Fix it BEFORE extracting, which does the whole lot at once:

      Right-click the ZIP  ->  Properties  ->  tick "Unblock"  ->  OK
      Then extract it.

  If you already extracted, right-click Overspray.dll and do the same thing.

  Still nothing? Two files say why:

      ScriptHookVDotNet.log      in the same folder as GTA5.exe
      scripts\Overspray\Overspray.log

  If Overspray.log does not exist AT ALL, the DLL never loaded -- that is the
  blocked-file problem above, or ScriptHookVDotNet is not installed. If it does
  exist, it says in plain English what went wrong, and that is the file to send.


-- REQUIREMENTS --

  Script Hook V              http://www.dev-c.com/gtav/scripthookv/
  ScriptHookVDotNet 3        github.com/scripthookvdotnet/scripthookvdotnet
  .NET Framework 4.8         (Windows 10 and 11 already have it)

  Version 3 of ScriptHookVDotNet. Not version 2. On GTA V Enhanced you need
  3.6 or newer.


-- INSTALLING --

  Copy the "scripts" folder from the zip into your GTA V folder -- the one with
  GTA5.exe in it -- and say yes to merging. That is the whole install.

  It must end up looking like this:

      Grand Theft Auto V\
        scripts\
          Overspray.dll
          Overspray.ini
          Overspray\
            icons\
              logo.png
              logo_0.png ... logo_7.png
              can.png
              cap_thin.png
              cap_stock.png
              cap_fat.png

  The folder inside a folder is deliberate. Do not flatten it.

  Script mods do NOT go in the OpenIV "mods" folder. They go in the real game
  folder.

  A few seconds after you spawn, Overspray posts a line in the top-left saying
  it loaded and which key opens it. If you see that, it is working.


-- CONTROLS --

  F3                open the picker
  LEFT / RIGHT      choose a colour, or a cap
  UP / DOWN         move between rows
  ENTER             take what is on the row
  BACKSPACE         close

  Then aim and hold the fire button.


-- SETTINGS --

Overspray.ini sits next to the DLL. Every line is commented and every setting
has a working default -- delete the whole file and the mod runs exactly the
same. Change the key, the reach, how dense the paint is, how much is remembered,
or turn the whole thing off.


-- KNOWN LIMIT, AND IT IS THE GAME'S --

GTA V only holds a few hundred decals in the entire world at once and quietly
drops the oldest. Overspray remembers everything you have painted and puts it
back as you approach, which is why walking away and returning brings it back --
but it cannot make the game hold more at one time. Raising that limit means
raising a cap this mod does not own. On GTA V Enhanced that is DecalPatch.asi,
a drop-in ASI with no OpenIV and no RPF editing -- its ini goes up to 2048, which
is about thirty seconds of solid spraying on screen instead of seven. On Legacy
it is a gameconfig with raised pools, installed with OpenIV, and it has to match
your game version; a mismatched one is the classic infinite loading screen, which
is why nothing here ships one.

Neither is required. It is a separate job and nothing to do
with this mod.


-- IF YOU ALSO RUN POSTED UP --

That mod has this built in as a Graffiti app on the phone, using the same
engine. Both installed is fine: Overspray notices and starts with its spray
switched off so the two do not paint over each other. F3 still opens, and you
can switch it on in there if you want both.
