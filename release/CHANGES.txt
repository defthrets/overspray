-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Unreleased
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE COLOURS COME OUT AS PICKED

Blood as the one texture for everything could not do it: the colour you pick
multiplies the texture, blood is authored red, and green came out bronze and
cyan grey. Paint takes colour honestly and is the texture for anything with a
colour in it again. Black is the one colour that does not care what the
texture is, and blood's shape is the best on the rack, so black keeps blood.
Two settings now: CanDecal for the colours, CanDecalDark for black.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0.4.1
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE CAN STOPPED AFTER HALF A LETTER, AND IT WAS THE EVICTION CHECK

0.4.0 asked the game whether each mark was still alive and learned a pool
budget from the answers. The probe it put up to prove the native honest
reported alive after it had been removed: IS_DECAL_ALIVE lags a frame or more
in both directions, so fresh paint read as evicted, the budget fell to its
floor in seconds, and the can was refused on every dab. The check, the budget
and the probe are gone. What stays from 0.4.0 is what was measured rather
than guessed: the wall fed twenty a frame, nearest first, and a refused dab
kept and tried again next frame -- ahead of everything else now, because it
is the paint in your hand.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0.4.0
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE MARK IS BLACKLETTER, AND THE PANEL IS TIDIED ROUND IT

Overspray in a Fraktur -- UnifrakturCook Bold, under the SIL Open Font
License, rendered to the same white mask the arched block was -- with paint
running off three of its strokes and spatter under the baseline. It still
sprays itself on when the panel opens, still glows in whatever is loaded,
still rocks. Taller, because a Fraktur's lowercase sits small inside its own
box. The rows under it lose their grey boxes and the sweeping sheen: a
hairline under each, and under the live one a soft fill and a rail in the
loaded colour. The footer's line of words is three key caps. The cap icons
are exactly as they were.

THE POOL THE COMPARES ARE NOT

With the thresholds at a hundred thousand the game still took paint down,
and without a word: its pool is a fixed number of slots, and when they are
gone the oldest decal goes -- yours as readily as its own -- and the handle
simply stops being alive. The record never checked. Now a slice of the marks
believed up is asked IS_DECAL_ALIVE every frame; a dead one is marked away,
counted, and if near goes straight back up next frame. The count that was up
when evictions started is the pool, and the engine keeps under it: once the
budget is reached, a far mark of ours gives up its slot BEFORE a new one is
asked for, so what goes is the furthest away rather than the oldest. The
budget creeps back while nothing is evicted, and the log says the number.

OLD TAGS COME BACK IN SECONDS, AND NOTHING IS TORN DOWN TO DO IT

The game takes about thirty-two new decals a frame. The sweep asked for every
away-mark in range in one frame every second and a half, read every refusal
as "the pool is full", and recycled a far mark's slot for each one -- six
thousand refusals a sweep was six thousand far marks wiped a sweep, which is
what "old tags disappear" was. The wall is now fed from a queue, twenty a
frame, what you are stood at first; a refusal after something has gone up
this frame is the intake and is simply tried again next frame; only a
refusal with nothing placed yet is the pool. Refused dabs from the can are
kept and come up next frame instead of being dropped.

THE CAN SPRAYS BLOOD, NEVER BULLETS

splatters_blood (1010) is the default texture now, on shape: a dense middle
with flung droplets round the edge, which is what comes off an aerosol. The
impact family -- 4010, 4020, 4050, the marks a bullet leaves -- is out of the
fallback ladder and refused wherever it is asked for: as the can texture, the
mix texture or the vehicle texture, blood is used instead and the log says so
once. The ladder is blood then paint and nothing else.


THE RECORD CAP IS A QUARTER OF A MILLION

MaxMarks was fifty thousand, set with a two-thousand-decal game pool in mind.
With DecalLimitAdjuster raising the pool the record is the only cap left, so
it is out of the way: 250,000 by default, up to two million. The refusal
warning names the adjuster and the pair it wants on Enhanced 1.0.1158.13.



-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0.3.0
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE LAW HAS OPINIONS ABOUT PAINT

An officer who sees you tagging arrests you. One star, and held at one star for
as long as a spray can is the worst thing in your hands -- because at one star
they cuff you and from two they draw, so "arrest him, do not kill him" is not a
behaviour to write, it is one star that refuses to climb.

Seen, not nearby. It wants a clear line of sight and an officer roughly facing
you; one the other side of the wall you are painting has not seen anything. A
patrol rolling past counts, which is the classic way to get caught at this.

Pull an actual gun and the cap comes off. What happens next is between you and
them. The fire extinguisher does not count as a gun, which the obvious version
of this check gets wrong -- it would book you for holding the very thing the mod
put in your hand.

And it only ever raises TO one star. Already wanted for something real and it
keeps out of it entirely: a man with three stars has bigger problems than a
wall, and quietly dropping him to one because he picked up a can would be the
mod rescuing him from the game.

CopsCare=false in the ini turns the whole thing off.


BIG PIECES COME BACK WHOLE

Two complaints, one missing line. Tags returned with chunks cut out of them, and
old ones eventually stopped returning at all.

Laying paint has always been able to reclaim a slot when the decal pool is full.
Restoring paint never asked. So walking up to a saved piece filled the pool with
whatever the list reached first -- and the list is oldest first, which is the
START of a tag -- and then every remaining mark failed silently, and failed again
on the next pass.

Measured rather than guessed: a save here holds 6,710 marks across four pieces,
the biggest of them 2,582, against a pool of 896 on a default Enhanced install.
Nothing was ever lost. What this fixes is WHICH marks get the slots, and the
answer is now the nearest ones. It also says so in the log when it cannot place
them all, once per change -- "some of my tag is missing" is impossible to act on
without a number.


STROKES ARE ONE STRETCHED MARK, NOT A CHAIN OF DOTS

ADD_DECAL takes a width and a height, and this engine handed it the same number
twice everywhere except the drips. One decal stretched along the path covers
what a string of round ones did, for a fraction of the marks -- which is the only
lever there is, because the pool size is the game's and not ours.

StreakStep in the ini is the trade: lower is more marks and a smoother line,
higher is fewer marks and a longer stretch of one texture. StrokeStreaks=false
is exactly what the mod did before, so it is one word to undo.


AND A FULL PACKAGE

There is a second zip now that carries ScriptHookVDotNet with it, so it merges
straight over a clean GTA V folder. ScriptHookV itself is not in it and cannot
be -- nothing in its package grants permission to hand it on, and it is pinned
to one game build besides. READ ME FIRST.txt in that zip covers it.

The mod-only zip is unchanged, for anybody who already runs script mods.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0.2.0
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE SPRAY COMES OUT OF HIS HAND

This was wrong for the whole time 0.1.0 was up, and it is the one to read.

A splatter grows with distance from the player, and that distance was taken from
the ped's position -- which is its ROOT, sitting on the floor between his feet.
So the nearest part of any wall was its skirting board and every mark fattened
all the way up it. The finest paint in every piece you ever sprayed was at ankle
height. It reads as a deliberate effect right up until you notice.

Measured from the hand bone it comes out right on its own, and symmetrically:
finest across the middle where he is actually working, opening up above his
reach and below it, which is what a can does, because that is exactly when the
can is furthest from the wall. Standing half a metre off a wall, 0.025 across
the middle out to 0.165 at the top -- against 0.028 at the ankles and 0.300
overhead before.

It is the bone the can is already attached to, so it moves with the animation.
Crouching, leaning and the first-person pose all come out right without any of
them being a special case.


CHROME AND GOLD

Thirteen colours, up from eleven.

Nothing in this game can make a decal reflective. A mark is a texture times one
tint and it lands as flat as the wall behind it, so chrome done as a grey tint
is primer and gold done as a yellow-brown tint is mustard -- which is what every
"metallic" that is really just a tint looks like.

What sells metal on a flat surface is that neighbouring marks DISAGREE. Some
land near white, some land gunmetal, and the eye assembles the scatter into a
reflection, because scatter is the only thing a reflection is ever caused by. So
a colour carries a sheen as well: the colour is the middle of a range and the
sheen is how far either side each mark may fall.

It costs no decals. The marks were being placed anyway; they are just no longer
all the same shade.


THREE CAPS

Thin, stock and fat, on their own row on the panel.

A cap sets the NARROWEST line the can can draw, not the widest, and that is the
whole of what a cap is. A fat cap cannot do fine work however close you hold it,
because the paint is already wide as it leaves the nozzle. How far off the wall
you stand still governs the far end, so the three converge as you step back --
which is correct rather than a shortcut. Half a metre off a wall the cap is the
only thing deciding the width; two metres off it is not the deciding factor any
more.

    standing 0.5 m off a wall      thin    stock   fat
      at his hands                 0.025   0.055   0.110
      half a metre above           0.036   0.055   0.110
      at the top of his reach      0.139   0.139   0.139


PAINT ON CARS

Reported: nothing shows on vehicles at all. The ray always hit them, the decal
was always placed, and nothing appeared -- because a car is not a wall as far as
the decal system is concerned. splatters_paint goes on the map perfectly and
does nothing on a panel.

But the game plainly CAN mark a car: bullet holes and blood land on one and stay
there while it drives, and those are the weapImpact family. So a hit on a
vehicle asks for weapImpact_metal instead, which is what a car panel is.

Unproven, which is why it is a setting and why the first mark to land on a car
writes a line either way. "Nothing happens on cars" with nothing in the log is
not something anybody can act on, including me.

Paint on a car is never saved -- the decal sits in world space, so putting it
back next session would put it in the middle of whatever road the car was on.


HOW MUCH WILL IT HOLD

At startup it reads your decal cap and says so, in one line, with roughly how
many seconds of solid spraying that is. It is the first question anybody asks
about a mod that covers walls and it should be answerable rather than guessable.

It does NOT write it. The file belongs to another author, editing somebody
else's config because it suits this mod is how a mod gets a reputation, and it
would not even work -- DecalPatch reads its ini when the ASI attaches, long
before any script exists, so a write from here lands a whole launch late.

The README stopped telling people to edit gameconfig, too. On Enhanced that is
the wrong answer: DecalPatch.asi is a drop-in that goes 512 / 768 / 896 / 1024 /
2048, no OpenIV and no RPF. Legacy still wants a gameconfig, and the README now
says why this mod will not ship one -- a gameconfig has to match the game build,
and a mismatched one is the classic infinite loading screen. That is not a thing
to hand strangers over a decal count.


FIRST PERSON

spray_can_male is authored for a camera behind him: it swings the arm across the
body, which is what painting a wall looks like from outside and is exactly wrong
from inside his own head. The can leaves the frame and wanders off to the left
while paint carries on appearing under a reticle it is nowhere near.

So in first person he holds the ready pose instead, can up and nozzle at the
wall. Only the arm changes; nothing about where the paint lands comes from the
animation and nothing about it was touched.


THE BLOCKED DLL

Three people reported the mod not loading, and the advice they were given was to
follow the install instructions carefully -- which does not help when that is not
the fault.

It is Windows marking files that came out of a downloaded zip. .NET refuses to
load a marked assembly, ScriptHookVDotNet reports an error, nothing appears in
game. It is the one fault the author cannot reproduce, because a DLL built on
your own machine is never marked.

Unblocking the zip before extracting is now step one of the README, ahead of
everything else, because that is what the reports actually are.


DRIPS, BUILT AND SHIPPED OFF

Hold the can on one spot and the paint runs. Two things are tracked: the dwell,
which decides there is too much paint there, and the run itself, which has its
own life and crawls down while the dwell continues. Move on and whatever was
running stops where it got to rather than following the reticle across the wall.
It thins as it goes and beads where it stops. It never starts on anything within
about twelve degrees of level, because paint does not run down a pavement.

Built twice -- dotted, then as one stretched decal -- and then switched off,
because it costs sixteen marks a run and the wall is the scarce thing. Off by
the flag rather than deleted: Drips=true in the ini brings it back, and the
stretched decal it taught us about is what 0.3.0's strokes are built on.


FIXES

Loading paint no longer looks like losing it. The load path called the same
routine as the CLEAR EVERY WALL button, which announces itself, so every startup
logged "Wiped every mark" one millisecond before "Loaded 192 marks". Nothing was
ever wiped, but that is not what it read like, and an hour went into a bug that
was not there.

Posted Up stopped stealing this mod's extinguisher. Both watch the same weapon
and engage on "is an extinguisher selected", so taking one here had the other
put a can in the same hand sixty-six milliseconds later. It was wrong on its own
terms too: an extinguisher picked up in a fire station silently became a can.

Denser paint for the same number of decals -- overlap, rate, and three density
levers, one of which was clamped out of existence and doing nothing at all.

The switch in the picker says MOD rather than SPRAY PAINT, and turning it off
still leaves F3 working. A switch that removes the way back is one you can only
undo by editing a file.

The wordmark is drawn rather than set, and sprays itself on.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0.1.0
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The first one anybody else could install.

Hold the trigger with a spray can out and whatever you are aiming at takes
paint. Walls, shutters, kerbs, the road, the side of a skip, a ceiling --
anything solid. Eleven colours. The paint stays where you put it and is still
there when you come back.

Press F3 for the can. Passers-by mouth off rather than run, because a can is not
a gun.
