===============================================================================
  OVERSPRAY 0.4.2  --  FULL PACKAGE
===============================================================================

  DRAG EVERYTHING IN THIS ZIP INTO YOUR GTA V FOLDER. SAY YES TO MERGING.

  Your GTA V folder is the one with GTA5.exe in it, normally:
  C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Grand Theft Auto V

  Then load a save and press F3.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  UNBLOCK THE ZIP BEFORE YOU EXTRACT IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Windows marks anything downloaded from the internet, and ScriptHookVDotNet
  refuses to load a marked DLL. It does not say so -- the mod is simply not
  there, with nothing in any log to explain it.

  Right-click the ZIP -> Properties -> tick "Unblock" -> OK. THEN extract.

  If you already extracted it, delete what you extracted, unblock the zip and
  extract it again.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  YOU STILL NEED ONE FILE. IT IS NOT IN HERE AND CANNOT BE.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ScriptHookV        http://www.dev-c.com/gtav/scripthookv/

  Nothing in its package grants anyone permission to hand it on, so no mod is
  allowed to include it -- and it is locked to one exact game build besides, so
  a copy bundled today would be the wrong one the week after the next patch.
  It takes about thirty seconds:

    1. Download from the link above.
    2. Open the zip, go into the "bin" folder.
    3. Drag ScriptHookV.dll and dinput8.dll into your GTA V folder.

  If ScriptHookV.dll is already sitting next to GTA5.exe -- and it is, if you
  run any .asi mod at all -- you have nothing to do. Skip this.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  WHAT IS IN HERE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  scripts\Overspray.dll       the mod
  scripts\Overspray.ini       every setting, commented
  scripts\Overspray\icons\    its artwork: the can, the three caps, the wordmark

  ScriptHookVDotNet.asi       ScriptHookVDotNet, which runs .NET mods. Included
  ScriptHookVDotNet2.dll      because its licence expressly allows it. Not our
  ScriptHookVDotNet3.dll      work -- (c) crosire, kagikn and Chiheb-Bacha.
  ScriptHookVDotNet.ini
  Licenses\                   their licence and notices, kept with the binaries

  README.txt                  how it works, controls, configuring it
  CHANGES.txt                 what is new

  ALREADY RUN OTHER .NET MODS? Then you already have ScriptHookVDotNet, and
  yours may well be newer than this copy. Every .NET mod on your machine shares
  one copy of it, so if in doubt, keep the newer one -- skip the four
  ScriptHookVDotNet files when merging and take only the scripts folder.

  If you would rather not be handed somebody else's copy at all, the mod-only
  zip is the same release without them.

  GTA V ENHANCED NEEDS 3.6 OR NEWER. The copy in here is fine. An older one you
  already have may load on Legacy only, and Overspray will simply not start.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THEN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Press F3. The can comes out and the wheel opens: thirteen colours, three cap
  sizes. Hold the trigger and whatever you are aiming at takes paint.

  Paint stays where you put it and is still there when you come back.

  Police who SEE you tagging will arrest you rather than shoot at you. Turn that
  off with CopsCare=false in Overspray.ini if you would rather paint in peace.

  Read README.txt for the rest.

  To uninstall: delete scripts\Overspray.dll, scripts\Overspray.ini and the
  scripts\Overspray folder. No game files are modified by this mod.
